## Resolution, and why it flatters nothing

At a real card width of 3.375in these work out to roughly 462, 236, 206 and 178
dpi respectively. A phone capture of a card filling the frame is typically
higher. So on resolution these specimens are, if anything, a **harder** test than
production capture — the opposite of the usual worry about clean benchmark data.

They are still easier than production in every other way: no glare, no skew, no
shadow, no motion blur. The Washington and Massachusetts cards do carry heavy
security overprint (a diagonal SAMPLE band, a dove hologram over the date block),
which is the closest thing here to real-world interference.
